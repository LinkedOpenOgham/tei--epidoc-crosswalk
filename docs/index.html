Linked Open Ogham

The TEI/EpiDoc editions of the OG(H)AM corpus, crosswalked to CIDOC CRM 7.1.3 and CRMtex. These pages are the browsable side of that graph: each one is generated from the same parse as the RDF, so a map and the triples behind it cannot disagree.

Axis 1 of the Linked Open Ogham crosswalk · source and RDF on GitHub

Findspot map

Where the inscribed stones were found, read out of <origPlace>/<geo> and crosswalked to CIDOC CRM E53_Place. Filterable by country; findspots the editors hedged are drawn as dashed rings.

Formulaic words

McManus's formulaic vocabulary matched against every reading of every inscription — so a word belongs to a reading and its editor, not to a stone. Picks up the DHd 2020 extractor on the successor corpus.

290stones with a match673occurrences104words searched

Where the editors disagree

Stones that carry more than one reading, coloured by how far the current edition sits from what an earlier editor saw. Filterable by editor, and by whether a formulaic word is what is at stake.

90stones contested138comparisons28with a formula word at stake

From findspot to museum

Stones that no longer stand where they were found, drawn as an arc from the findspot to the institution that holds them now. The institutions are geocoded against Wikidata and OpenStreetMap.
